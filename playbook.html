HOW TO USE
▸ 7v7 Playbook Creator ◂
1

MOVE PLAYERS

In 🖐 MOVE mode, click & drag any player to reposition them.

2

DRAW ROUTES

Switch to ✏️ DRAW mode, then click & drag from a player to freehand their route. Release to save.

3

SWITCH MODES

Click MOVE / DRAW toggle in sidebar, or press R on keyboard.

4

DELETE & UNDO

Right-click any player to delete their route or reset position. Ctrl+Z to undo.

5

EXPORT & SHARE

Export as PNG / PDF, copy to clipboard, or generate a share link. Use tabs for multiple plays.
