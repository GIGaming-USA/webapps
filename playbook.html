HOW TO USE
▸ 7v7 Playbook Creator ◂
1

MOVE PLAYERS

In 🖐 MOVE mode, tap & drag any player to reposition.

2

DRAW ROUTES

Switch to ✏️ DRAW, then drag from a player to draw their route. Release to save.

3

SWITCH MODES

Tap the MOVE / DRAW toggle, or press R on keyboard.

4

DELETE & UNDO

Long-press or right-click a player to delete route or reset. Undo button to go back.

5

EXPORT & SHARE

Open Tools panel below for PNG, PDF, clipboard, and share link.
